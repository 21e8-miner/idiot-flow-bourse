Methodology & Caveats

Honest accounting of what this replay does and does not capture
  • Strict zero-lookahead causality: At every closed 1h bar t, compute_pine_metrics receives only candles[:t+1]. The final still-forming bar is dropped before replay.
  • Execution model: Close-to-close fills (matching Pine process_orders_on_close = true) with per-symbol cooldown_hours to prevent overlapping correlated bar-after-bar triggers.
  • Cost model: Round-trip fee + slippage (0 / 10 / 20 bps) deducted linearly from each trade's gross return. Funding rates and partial fills are not modeled.
  • Universe caveat: Fixed liquid major universe introduces mild survivorship/selection bias relative to illiquid altcoins where retail 24h-tile chasing may behave differently.
  • Pine window-extreme note: engine.py checks whether the outgoing bar is the largest bar in the current 24h display window [t-23..t], whereas RJ_24H_Roll_Off_Adaptive_v2.pine shifts ta.highest(barReturn, 24)[23] to [t-46..t-23].
